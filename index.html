ABOUT

Emma Zhong · 仲曼琳

I’m a Chinese woman who spent her formative years — high school and university — in Canada. Living across cultures sharpened my interest in how societies shape the way people think, feel, and understand themselves.

I read across history, psychology, and politics, and I often return to philosophy, education, and art as ways of thinking through questions of human nature, meaning, memory, and becoming.

My writing begins with personal experience and extends into family memory, social observation, and academic inquiry.
