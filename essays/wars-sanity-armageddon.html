诗歌：Wars, Sanity, and Armageddon

2026. 3. 7

Wars, Sanity, and Armageddon

The emotions brim,

tipped over the threshold of life.

The container, my page, is soaked wet,

taking in drips of life.

It takes such naivety, such an artless heart to ask:

Will it all be fine?

Wars, massacres, and suffering are perpetual conditions of human life.

I seem to be waiting in secrecy,

for something ominous to befall.

What to do,

the millennial question.

Worry less, people say.

Ignorance is not bliss.

Worry is interest paid on trouble before it falls due.

One for the city dwellers, one for you:

One food-shelf away from famine,

hasn’t it always been?

Have our screens frayed all minds?

Pepper flakes of humility fall,

sacrificially into a melting pot.

One that assimilates all civilizations into ashes.

Too dead to live, too alive to die.

“Say when,”

the pepper girl prompts.

Pretension is weak as ever;

Mice scurry in the flickering eyes behind the window panel.

The morning sunrise staunchly composed;

Yet loses its lure to the depressed souls.

Sane this second, delirious the next.

The unadulterated life eschews us all.
