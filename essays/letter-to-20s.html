散文：A Letter to Our 20s

2024. 3. 11

I wrote a letter to my 20’s self. Having adapted it for your understanding, here it shares:

    —

          ... This is a question of my constant, conscious interrogation.

          What are your values to uphold in this life, in this world? Numerous bullets to shot at different stages and states. That of a frontiers woman is for today. I made a conscious choice of setting my heart to the land of no mercy.

          There, the river doesn’t care if you can swim. The snake doesn’t care how much you love your parents; and the wolf has no interest in your dreams. If you fail to beat the current, you will drown. If you get too close, you will be bitten. If you are too weak, you will be eaten. Each and every single actions have consequences in the land of no mercy. There can be no mistakes — because here doesn't care.

          In no way do I intend to mean it as a capitalist or Darwinist manifesto. The life at frontier operates under the 'matriarchal spirituality', characterized by a sharpened sensitivity of each fleeting present, where the innocence, the beast from within unleashes, the explosives experiment. It is a life of spontaneity and ultimately, full-fledged responsibility in the slippery slopes of life and death. At the end of the day, I dream of survival — the independence and the self-reliance of my soul and material, where I have painstakingly taken every stride in the climb to be readied for life.

          There can be no excuses, I don't dare waste away my precious life, the once-in-a-lifetime window of my 20s. There were moments of heartbreak, where I fell on my knees in ill, yet I stand up — I cannot imagine otherwise, dribbling on is what we all ought to do. True sufferings mute us: in the dictation of silence, disoriented women struggle in tentation, matured in discomforts, grew somewhat thorough, content, determined.

          We need a synthesis of both the matriarchal and patriarchal spirituality to mature, to become our own parents. The patriarchal spirituality is a civilized, city one — man lives ahead of himself, clocks in and clocks out according to law and order, at all times. Tomorrow is where life, the insatiability of the heart directs, not today. City life is said by Elsa Dutton to have weakened us as a species:

          'Step into the road without looking and the carriage nearly stops or swerves; the only consequence, an angry driver. ’

          The synthesis of matriarchal and patriarchal spirituality is inculcated in man by his mother and father — he lives in, and for, both today and tomorrow. Paradoxically, how? — Man is simply grounded by the perpetual tension, like the force of gravity pulling him to stay on earth and not to fly out to an slanted, outlandish space short of air for his fundamental human needs. A union is to be reached from within us before it spills over to the world, before we can properly bond, and reach the union with others.

          I contemplate what I am here for. Man has transcended his animal nature though he never leaves it. Yet once thrown out of the paradise — the state of oneness with nature — he can never return to it. History only moves forward, we move forward through integrating our nature with productive reason to find a new, human harmony. This is how we fix the problem of separateness, endowed by our self-consciousness as a separate entity in the world having been gifted or cursed by reason. There might not be a meaning for "being", but are numerous modes to it. This is a tip of iceberg to drink. Life is, and will be a consequence of our compounded decisions, either conscious, active, autonomous, or not.

          I desire freedom, I shouted, I urged for air to breathe. It is time for seriousness for us all. [2024.3.11]

          —
