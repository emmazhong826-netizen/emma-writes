诗歌：Not Your Crucifixion, Just My Disappointments

2026. 4. 7

Not Your Crucifixion, Just My Disappointments How easy it is to promise, to soothe a restless soul; How tenuous the road is, demanding the man of action his last strength, and more. How long can lovers tangle— between the space of seamless touches and ephemeral warmth? How sincere can pretentious care last— before taken over by the mantra of lament? “I’m sorry” is all they say. The kisses are tender and deep— as deep as his nonchalance as he turned his back on me; It halves my soul as deep as his figures do, to my body. Sun-kissed morning, fragrance, and hope; The wars stink afar. Coffeeshop, a bus driver; Capricious eyes, capacious laughter— confined to a hunched seat from nine to five. I take a sip, the aroma mellow; churning out drinkers of meek souls. My croissant is crunchy; My peace, bittersweet. In an agonizing night of thoughts, I feel a permanent loneliness, decorated by meteorites of tears. In vanity, I doomed myself to an impossibility— ... . . .
"Always Look on the Bright Side of Life" is the final song from the 1979 comedy film "Monty Python's Life of Brian". The song was written and performed by Eric Idle, who appears in the film as a prisoner hanging on a cross next to the protagonist, Brian.
