散文：知识的诅咒

2025. 8. 15

I found that there is a curse that comes with too much knowledge.

I have started to process everything sensibly through a symbolic lens.

I had almost lost my capacity to appreciate the beauty in a parent’s love towards the child.

I have elevated my expectations to a historic level, and I only appreciate work that is the product of both good intentions and effortful work.

I was simply long gone into my own criteria.

I had fixated my own value on something that had almost sedimented into a prejudice within myself.

I never leave my own mind.

Even when I do, I don’t.

I had somehow become the adult in the room.

There’s much thinking to be done there.

I had experienced a time where my own heart was as soft and as vulnerable as a flower petal.

I had also experienced a time—which is now—where my heart had become numb.

Not really.

It’s not numb, but it is no longer led by my overriding emotions. It has been flattened by constant interruptions from life.

To delineate all this confusion:

I have had a time where I was unknowingly weak, but my heart had been hardened.

It is here, at the word “hardened”, that I may have formed a conclusion that is askew.

I’m not hardened.

On the contrary, I’m extremely soft and prone to tears from any form of literary beauty.

However, I always have this kind of vigilance around people.

I never trust people fully, and I’m never at ease so easily.

I don’t know when the right time is to trust.

I’ll need to figure that out as well.

I know that I can’t hold on to the dark belief that we are all destined to our own desolation.

This principle may ring truer than any other one.

However, lonely people can also get together and have a carnival soberly, no?

You’re misunderstood but loved by ignorant people.

I can’t have control at all times.

I’ll need to allow myself to be affected, having ensured that I’m in a safe environment.

I think that this is the reason why we all need to feel safe and become kids once in a while.

Otherwise, life becomes such a burden to bear.

It feels GREAT to write.

It feels liberating to enter a whole new world.

It feels wonderful to become serious and free.

I don’t want to be a sad person.

I’d like to be a happy person.

I’d like to bring hope to the world and to people around me.

I’m a woman in action.

I had constantly sought a depressed state, and I knew it for sure.

I was constantly trying to be unhappy to soothe myself.

But perhaps life is not that complicated.

There are things to do.

I do them.

And I take my time to enjoy the rest—

the time with endearing family,

the time to read,

and the time to do whatever.

Perhaps.

Indeed.
