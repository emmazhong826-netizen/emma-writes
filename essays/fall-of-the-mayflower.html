诗歌：The Fall of the Mayflower

2026. 8. 7

The more I downplay the memories, the more they burn. I wonder if writing a poem is overly sentimental, poking the air, figuring something out of nothing. He has moved on with his life. All the heart that I poured has become a light trace of memory. Maybe he’ll learn. Maybe he never will. I know I am special. I've always mourned for others, the pitiful losses in our world, beautiful flowers that didn’t come to fruition. It’s time to mourn for myself. I’m not unfazed, but it’ll take some time to understand my feelings. Is he after the truth? Is he after love? Or is he an aimless wanderer who doesn’t know better yet? I’m a fighter for love. I fought for him with all that is there in me. I love to win. I don’t love in the fear of losing. Our love has no roots. Our love has no fruits. And I accept the fall of the Mayflower.
