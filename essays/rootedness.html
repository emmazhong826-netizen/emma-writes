散文：扎根

2026. 9. 19

Something rocky, onerous, mystic, and heavy as Mount Everest was laid bare in front of me.

It was about who I am.

It is about my family.

It is about re-rooting myself.

I have always questioned what “rootedness” means.

I realized that rootedness means to inherit and continue a culture that was built by my own family, brick by brick.

I have always wondered what the legacy of my family is.

I feel that the air was so thin, the communication so barren and fragmented that there is barely any discourse happening within the household at all.

However, perhaps there are other gestures of love.

Perhaps I can be the inheritor and the expander, the pioneer to implant a new language of love into my own family.

It is a language of literacy, of striving not simply towards material comfort, but also towards self-actualization.

It is a complicated question, those about the meaning of self-actualization, of devoting all that is within you towards a noble goal.

Is that noble goal “Your Goal”?

What does it mean to give it all?

Wouldn’t we need to patiently wait for that calling to arrive then?

If not, how can we initiate a discovery?

It is difficult.

Life has truly exposed my plight to myself.

Predictability provides psychological safety, but it’s not the same as human flourishing.

I can grow out of the predictable familial pattern and draw a new path.

Perhaps this is also what rootedness means.

It is not simply returning to what already exists.

It is inheriting what was given to us, understanding it, and then expanding it.

I can be the inheritor.

I can also be the person who adds a new language.
