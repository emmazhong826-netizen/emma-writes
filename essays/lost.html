诗歌：Lost

日期未记

I want to be alone.

When the only thing you do

is say the wrong thing

at the wrong time,

I can’t stand this.

When the only things you produce

are embarrassment and frustration,

I feel miserable.

I want to shut myself down.

I have the purest, sincerest heart,

but none of it can reveal itself

through this impropriety—

between what is said and not meant,

what is meant and not said.

Most love is gone.

The most pathetic thing on earth

is no more than

the inability of self-expression—

the expression

of my transparent heart,

unafraid of speculation,

yet shackled

by its sweetest childhood recollections

and Mom’s reserved caress.

One has to be lost

in order to be found.

I only begin to reflect and change

when I feel crushed.

Worried, scared,

tired of feeling these emotions,

I’m desperate for an escape.

But that is indeed the direction

I would never turn,

for they constitute

the deepest part of my heart,

yet they keep draining

my energy away and away.

Life is filled with ups and downs.

This is a universal road

we are all destined

to set foot on.

Growth happens in the unspoken,

and in what cannot be spoken.

It is something

that can only be overcome

through trials and action.

So I’m ready.
