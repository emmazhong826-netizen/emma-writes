杂记：Vancouver State of Mind

2026. 9. 1

Tonight, I felt peace seeping into me.

Life is a train, indeed, and we often lose ourselves in the trivialities that aren’t important.

I was so absorbed in some moments—the moments where I was outside of this room, or even outside of myself.

I was disoriented.

And now I’m re-orienting myself.

I’m alive again.

I know the girl that I was, and I will not let her down.

That Emma.

The Emma who was with her friends—████████████████████████████, and the list goes on.

I feel so blessed to have had my overseas experience.

It hit me like a meteorite hitting the ocean, throwing it onto the sky, turning it upside down.

I feel I’m ready, and my attention is directed outward for the first time.

I know that I am not ordinary.

I can be extraordinary.

Maybe.

I will not let myself down.

I’m proud of you, Emma, to have been able to adjust yourself to your current state of mind.

It’s not a New York State of Mind.

It’s a Vancouver State of Mind.

I just want to write something.

What should I write?

Perhaps I should write a book.

Make it more systematic.

I have simply fallen in love with my own words. I’m not sure if it is in the same way that Narcissus falls in love with his own reflection.

However, I do believe in the magic of these words to evoke concrete changes in this world.

Perhaps I should write something.

It has been so long since I had a regimented life where I could experience intellectual stimulation on a concrete basis.

It requires both a physical and psychological holding environment.

So what should I write?

It’s a spiritual anchor that I set aside for my own life.

There is simply no way that I will give up.

However, by this moment, I have also understood an important lesson:

I must make it more systematic.

When there is an external gaze, I have a tendency to make my writing more romantic than it needs to be.
