What was the state that I was in? I call it “emotional drift”. It is a state of mindless wandering. It’s compulsive dwelling, if not self-absorption, combined with degrees of self-criticism. It is unproductive to say the least. It was a mentality that I slid into when there are no clearly defined objectives or prospects. I think I was just demonstrating the temporary inability to bear uncertainty.

And then, it gets better. I come back to my senses and composure. I reclaim my intention and clarity.

There are so many things to do now: I have my French language study going on (three times a week); I’m teaching English on the side to earn extra money (I bought a kitten today, I need to earn even more money to afford our life!); I’m helping my parents with their business; and most important of all, my university applications! Oh and, I’d love to explore AI in combination with my creative instincts to establish an operational business model.

Life is packed, but I know how to thrive in this mosaic mess. It is all about feeling, and essentially sustaining, a euphoria that was brought forth by the right actions. I achieved this in the afternoon. Literature also helped tremendously. It is the citadel of my own mind and creates distance between myself and reality, which my own sensitive, fretful heart can eat no more at times.
