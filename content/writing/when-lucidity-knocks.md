When Lucidity Knocks

The soil that I come out of has gone stale.
The root, infected, stinks.

I was uprooted, a long while ago.
My parents had a few coins,
    to test my odds of escaping a killing that no one comes out unscathed.

I make it a mission to plant myself in a spiritual desert, and survive.
I had long stopped feeling apologetic about myself.
I had a heart of a peach, a backbone of steel, and burning eyes that lit my own soul on fire, if not the world.
I exude,
    sometimes deflation, sometimes utter reckless positivity.

In a moment like this,
    when lucidity slowly comes back to me.
I ask myself,
    what do you live for?
Some day it feels,
There are no one, no goal, no meaning, nothing to live for whatsoever.

It’s not true.
I laugh at myself.
Aren’t I savoring a moment of radical freedom now?
What luck have you signed yourself,
    to have a minute of peaceful heart as such?
What a crazy cushion of awareness to dwell on in a volatile world.

Then I start to admire the writers again,
    who dare to make a life assembling words.
    to salvage their barren lives through creating another world.
Perhaps have even saved a few more others, with their human stories.
The cherries blossom,
I re-collect the dust of hope.

The world owes me nothing,
I owe the world something,
    especially to the strangers who showed me charity.
Many people expect from me.
I reciprocate the sincere wishes that expect a better self from me,
    who don’t demand an alienated Other to come out.
May sanity stays with me.
