A Night of Stars

Those are acute eyes,

Perspicacious to worldly subtleties.

Trinkling judgements,

Theorin fates sealed by the stars.

Closing the pretty eyes,

Love befalls in wisdom’s surrender.

The impervious soldier boy,

Embrace his tender, invincible defeat.

Light gets in where there is a crack.

Burnt grass rebirth from a root intact.

We all suffered, wronged,

and mellowed down with a crescent smile in the eyes.
