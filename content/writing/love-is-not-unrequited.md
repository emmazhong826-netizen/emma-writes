Love is not unrequited

His outpour of emotional nudity distresses me,

       who is yet to recognize the man’s precious invitation for me to love.

Rid of the façade of omnipotence is the genuine self,

       an admirable character of incompleteness, indignation, yearning.

The universal man—we adorn, we are.

Yet comes our unrelenting fixture on the ego, too firm and fearful to let loose.

In the hindsight,

I contemplate the odds of us co-charting the world,

where the mind wanders again and again.

Yet,

Drips from a heart’s incise don’t dangle,

Un-reciprocated love renounces in freezing silence.

Something irreducible in him escapes me.

He escaped me.

In memory,

I resented my impotence, here and there.

23/11/2024
