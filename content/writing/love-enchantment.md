LOVE ENCHANTMENT

Preface:

Lost, infatuated, and the memories meandering.

I ruminate on my love experience compulsively.

I turn poetries into preservatives of our sweet time together.

Poetries dedicated to my man, our time, and love.

Turn on the music, and enjoy the selected pieces.

(2) Learn to Carry Love

I lost my lover.

I will not “move on”;

I cannot ever.

I learn to carry love’s crushing weight without losing myself.

Lingering feelings occasionally overwhelm me.

I turn them to hard work —

a form of devotion to his legacy.

I am here,

and he is gone.

May the exchange of “see you soon” stand;

I will meet you at a better place.

I must fight at the battalion of reality now.

(4) He Becomes Part of Me

He has become an integral part of me.

He gave me a spiritual home in the loveless city.

I melt and find myself in his embrace.

His love irrigates me,

making me stronger.

City life is so cruel and grinding;

          Its crashing waves etched my porous heart.

He is a furnace that temporarily fends me against darkness.

I beam with new breath and new humanity.

(5) Sway

I used to sway with angst,

I now sway with vitality, jazziness, and equanimity.

The rhythmic horns of life shifted,

from Eminem’s discombobulation to Omar’s legendary enchantment for love.

(6) I Admire Him

I admire his high moral standing, manly reservedness, and serious masculinity.

I admire his contemplative nature, humane sensitivity, and caring humor.

He has listened to the world.

I want to be his listener.

I want to listen to him talking about his families, which —

beautifully, is all that he talks about.

(10) In His Arms

He touched my body with a little nerves —

      with measurable respect, patience, self-constraint, and controlled eroticism.

He followed my demands with adamancy.

My soul was destabilized and reinvented in his arms.

I am an entranced woman.

In tenderness,

       he claimed my heart.

(12) Olfactory Memory

The bedding smells like him;

       my nose caresses him.

I bury my face in weariness,

       imagining his presence.

Dizzy euphoria washes over every inch of my body.

— The End —
