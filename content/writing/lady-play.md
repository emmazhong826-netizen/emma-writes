Lady Play

He seeks the mask,
        I want the man beneath it.
He seeks the thrills, seduction, and epiphany—
        The performance, in a word.
I want the truth, blatant and imperfect.

He doesn’t understand,
Anyone can play the 'lady proper'—
        sealed off, sexy, unreadable, bland-faced.
        An effortless pose.
The lady knows how to want.

The woman knows how to give.
She offers her artless heart, with both hands.
Tragic when the event's rarily goes unnoticed.
Taking it for granted,
        He lost the privilege  to see it again.

To be a man is to see her—
not the script.
To read the feelings in her
and hold it steady.
Until,
           she feels seen, safe, and wanted.
This is what sophistication is for.

A man may have the heart of a rock.
But a little opening,
     courageous enough to take on her joy and sorrow.
