Work Log 00: Preface

2026. 10. 1

This work log cannot help but become brimmed with emotions, compulsive contortions, truthful observations, and constructive criticisms.

I have come to understand myself after all these years. It is not difficult to get myself out of my unsettledness and devote my great energy to something good—to anything I believe in. Once I do, I pounce on a project like a black panther.

However, all of this hinges on the right emotional state, one that seems almost osmosed from the right environment.

I was not held or truly seen as a child, and I demand stability, patience, and genuine recognition in interpersonal relationships. When these are deprived, I fall into immediate and immense self-doubt and torment that fuzz with my work, if not my endearing life.

This work journal will not only contain professional observations about the educational domain in China. Even more so, I will truthfully detail the texture of my work: the little progress across various projects, our marketing and course design, and the sparks of interpersonal intimacy within the company.

I will try my best to be truthful.

Yet I have to acknowledge that, despite my best efforts to feel “right,” I simply feel how I feel. I would not alter these personal annotations, or edit out the tendencies they reveal, from my records.

It is my work log, at the end of the day.

This blog has a momentary power. It allows me to recalibrate my work through “narrative power”: to stop, look back, give form to what has happened, and then return to the work with greater clarity.

I’m looking forward to the chapters to come.
