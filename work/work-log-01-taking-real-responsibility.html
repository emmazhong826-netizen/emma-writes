Work Log 01: Taking Real Responsibility

2026. 10. 1

I have always told myself, it is time to take real responsibility.

While learning how to get a hang of work, I also slowly learned, knowing that I have the potential to do something and “actually doing something” are two fundamentally different things. The former is everyone’s pitiful unrealized dreams, the latter is a concrete outcome to be proud of.

I set out my mind to complete the latter — to prove real impacts in real societies, which actually, is my first attempt outside of the buffered campus environment.

As for how to do this, I had realized one thing first and foremost. I can’t be overly absorbed into work. I can’t work 24/7 relentlessly. I need some rest, be it high-quality time with my family, the solitary comfort of the literature world, or simply self-forgeting writings and local tours. I needed breathing space. I had worked like a beast, with my sleep compromised, and I loathed my panda eyes that were formed in the past month. Therefore, work—but with extreme focus during the block but intentional restraint overall.

Second, I’m slowly learning about establishing a work relation with my colleagues. They are of diverse personalities. Brandon is the only colleague that I physically work with in Houjie, Dongguang, Guangzhou. I don’t think I know him well, and he definitely does not understand me in the slightest. However, I don’t think work is about seamless understanding—it’s an ideal worthy of constant striving—it is about becoming a reliable person when called upon, or even when not called upon. It is about hardening myself against difficulty and excuses in life to implement the responsibilities whatsoever. I’m still yet to understand how he conceives this work—he’s a sensitive person, but not communicative at all. It is therefore bestowed upon me to observe more.

For now, I will work on a few things. To promote the Ling Duo Night is my first priority among other work items. To build towards it, I will first finalize the website, which the invitation card QR code directly lead to, so that I can start to ran coffee shops and discuss the prospect for collaboration with the local owners. Second, I will start to write a basic outline, not verbatim, about the procedures and scripts that I can follow in these negotiations and talk to Brandon about it (together, I will send to Brandon about the selections of local cafes and corresponding reasons). Third, I’ll head out to talk to local cafes, let’s make two cafes the goal, and I will write a report immediately.

In the evening, I will send out the “Ling Duo Night Rednote promotional post”. I will study the timing of buying views and settle the time to purchase them (be it today or next week). I will also communicate with the Wechat Subscription Account Dongguanbao to pay for promotional efforts.
